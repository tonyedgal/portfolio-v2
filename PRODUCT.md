## Purpose

## Content and navigation
Experience is the default route. Projects, Blog, and Story have separate routes. Identity and contact links remain in the left panel on desktop. The existing navbar is centered at the bottom of the left sidebar. On mobile, the navbar stays fixed at the bottom while the introduction and MDX content scroll. Reserve space below content so final lines and links can scroll above the navbar, including the bottom safe area. Section navigation scrolls smoothly, with an instant alternative for reduced motion.
